Common questions

What is a PC bottleneck?

A workload-specific performance limit where one component constrains another.

Does resolution change bottlenecks?

Yes. Higher resolution generally increases graphics workload, while high FPS targets can expose CPU limits.

How do I verify a result?

Check CPU per-core usage, GPU utilization, frame time, temperatures and clock speeds during the real workload.
