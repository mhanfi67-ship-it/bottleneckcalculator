About us

Better PC decisions, with clearer information.

Bottleneck Calculator was created to make PC performance questions easier to understand before you buy, build or upgrade.

Our purpose

A CPU or GPU specification does not tell the complete performance story. Resolution, workload, memory, cooling, storage and power delivery all influence the experience. Our goal is to turn those relationships into understandable planning guidance.

What the calculator evaluates

The calculator compares selected CPU and GPU capability, then adjusts the comparison for resolution and intended workload. It also considers RAM capacity, storage type, peak temperature and estimated PSU headroom.

The result is an educational estimate rather than a laboratory benchmark. Real performance varies with games, applications, settings, drivers, cooling, background software and component condition.

Content standards

  • Explain estimates and limitations clearly.
  • Prefer practical monitoring advice over a single percentage.
  • Keep technical explanations understandable without removing important context.
  • Review meaningful corrections and update inaccurate information.
  • Separate advertising from editorial explanations.

Who it is for

The site is intended for gamers, PC builders, students, creators and everyday users who want to understand a performance limitation or plan an upgrade.

Contact and corrections

If you find an incorrect hardware entry or a technical error, please contact us with the relevant page and supporting information.
